**Signature fragment** (first 41 nt):
```

```

**Classification**: `Eukaryotic Core Promoter with TATA Box & HindIII Site`

**Analysis**: Characterized by a canonical Goldberg-Hogness TATA box
(TATAAA) flanked by high-GC clamp regions, an architecture recognized by
transcription factor IID (TFIID) and TATA-binding protein (TBP). This
complex nucleates pre-initiation complex (PIC) positioning ~25–30 bp
upstream of transcription start, while the flanking GC clamps stabilize
promoter conformation. The presence of a downstream HindIII restriction
cleavage site (AAGCTT) indicates an engineered promoter-reporter cassette
or expression cloning vector.

**Signature** (full sequence):
```

```

**Classification**: `Hyper-GC Genomic Island / CpG Regulatory Element`

**Analysis**: Exhibits an extreme GC fraction of ~88%, diagnostic of dense

secondary structures such as G-quadruplexes. In cellular chromatin, these
